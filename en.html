HOW WE CAN HELP

Empathy first.
Technology as support.

No device replaces presence, warmth and understanding. Only when we know what you are really facing do we choose tools that will genuinely help you.

PSYCHOTHERAPY

Therapy for adults and couples

Anxiety, overload, low mood, a life crisis or difficulties in a relationship. Sessions with Daria in English, in our Wrocław office or online.

FEARS AND PHOBIAS

Small steps, more freedom

Fear of flying, spiders or other situations. Your fear is real and deserves respect. With VR we can practice gradually, in a safe and controlled setting, at your own pace.

ADHD AND LEARNING

For children, students and adults

ADHD, dyscalculia and learning difficulties. We help you understand how you or your child learns and find strategies that work, together with parents.

EXPERT CONSULTATIONS

Sebastian Kraszewski
